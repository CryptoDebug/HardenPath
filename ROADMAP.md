# HardenPath roadmap

HardenPath currently covers 35 beginner modules and 18 intermediate modules across ten learning paths. Advanced modules are intentionally not advertised in filters until content exists for the selected path.

## Near term

- Validate assessment quality with reviewed question banks and attempt analytics.
- Add instructor review workflows for written exam submissions.
- Complete account verification, privacy, and self-service lifecycle flows.
- Harden self-hosting, backups, monitoring, and upgrade documentation.

## Intermediate

- Expand guided labs with reproducible, isolated environments.
- Add intermediate exams after their prerequisites and rubrics are reviewed.
- Introduce subscription-provider integration without coupling course access to one vendor.

## Advanced

- Publish advanced paths only after threat modeling, technical review, and ethical review.
- Add certificates only when identity, assessment integrity, revocation, and audit history are ready.

Roadmap entries describe direction, not delivery dates or entitlement promises.
